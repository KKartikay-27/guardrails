# Refunds and cancellation

You can cancel at any time from Settings > Billing > Cancel plan. Your workspace stays on the paid plan until the end of the current billing period, then moves to Free.

Annual plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase. After 14 days, annual plans are not refundable but you keep access until the term ends. Monthly plans are not refunded for partial months.

To request a refund, the workspace owner must email billing@northwind.example from the owner's account email address, including the workspace URL.
