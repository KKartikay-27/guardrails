# Plans and billing

Northwind has three plans. Free: up to 3 projects and 5 members. Team: $12 per member per month billed monthly, or $10 per member per month billed annually, with unlimited projects. Enterprise: custom pricing, adds SAML SSO, audit logs, and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month and sent to the billing contact. Workspace owners can change the billing contact under Settings > Billing > Billing contact. We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and bank transfer (Enterprise only).

Adding a member mid-cycle is prorated to the day. Removing a member credits the unused days to your next invoice; credits are not paid out as cash.
