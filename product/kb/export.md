# Exporting data

Any member can export a single project as CSV from the project menu > Export. Workspace owners can export the whole workspace as JSON from Settings > Data > Export workspace. Workspace exports are prepared in the background and a download link is emailed to the owner; the link expires after 7 days.

Attachments are not included in CSV exports. JSON workspace exports include attachments as signed URLs that are valid for 7 days.

Deleted projects stay in the trash for 30 days and can be restored by any workspace admin. After 30 days they are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered by support.
